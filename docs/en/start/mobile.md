# Mobile version

<p class="hds-lede">The cloud Studio opens in a phone browser: you can sign in, look through your projects, accept an invitation and answer an inbox item. Real work on documents is still more comfortable on a computer.</p>

## What is supported

- **Browsers**: Safari on iOS and Chrome on Android, recent versions, screens from 360 px wide.
- **Tablets** (768 px and up) get the same interface as a computer, with finger-sized input fields (Safari does not zoom the page on focus).
- **Themes and languages**: light, dark and system, Russian and English. On a phone you can switch the theme and the language in the account menu (the circle with your initials at the top right).
- **Notch and home indicator** of an iPhone are taken into account, and the on-screen keyboard does not cover the field you type in.

## What works on a phone

- sign-in, registration by invitation, password reset, password and passkey sign-in;
- the landing page of the cloud and invitations (to the installation and to a project);
- the project list: search, filters and sorting in a sheet, the "New project" button, opening a project;
- the inbox and the bell with notifications;
- the account menu: settings, help, the AI assistant, theme and language, sign-out;
- the "Connect Studio" and "Local version" screens;
- the project workbench: a bottom tab bar (documents, issues, submission, files and "More" with project settings, history and the agent), the document list, the overview, project settings, submission, history, files;
- a document: the PDF preview fitted to the screen with zoom by buttons and by pinching, the text editor with a scrolling toolbar, the outline over the text and a larger font (Safari does not zoom the page while you type); the header and the tab bar hide while you type;
- people in the project, the connected Studios badge and the sync state in the second row of the project header: a tap opens a sheet;
- the agent on the full screen.

Dialogs, menus and lists of actions open as a sheet from the bottom: close it with the cross, with "Cancel" or by tapping the dimmed background.

## What is still easier on a computer

- the "Side by side" mode (preview and editor next to each other): it is not available on a phone, there is a switch between them;
- system settings and the admin console;
- the ONLYOFFICE editor.

These screens open on a phone too but are designed for a wide screen. We move them to the phone one by one.

## If something does not fit

Send a screenshot and the device name through "Report a problem" on the error page or in the help menu.
