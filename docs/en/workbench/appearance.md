# Appearance and text size

<p class="hds-lede">Settings, Appearance holds the theme, density, language and text size. Text size changes fonts, spacing, button and field heights and icons across the whole interface at once.</p>

## Text size

Five steps: 90%, 100% (default), 112%, 125% and 140%. The choice applies immediately, no reload needed. A sample with a heading, a paragraph, a button and a caption sits under the switch so you can see how the text will look.

- **What grows.** Menus, lists, forms, buttons, dialogs and phone sheets, project panels, the admin console, the landing page, the source editor and the visual editor. Spacing and heights grow with the text, icons too, and touch targets never get smaller.
- **What stays the same.** The content of a document and the page scale in the PDF preview. The page zoom of the visual editor is set separately, as before.
- **Where it is stored.** In your account and in the browser. The browser copy lets the page open at the right size straight away, with no jump after loading; the account carries the choice to another device. While a device is on the default size, signing in picks up the account value.
- **Large browser font.** The browser's "Font size" setting no longer stretches the interface on top of the chosen step; browser page zoom (Ctrl and plus) works as before.

## Low vision and small screens

125% or 140% helps when the text is too small to read; 90% fits more on screen. In a narrow window at a large step the layout switches to the phone form later than at 100%, because window width is counted in ordinary pixels. If things do not fit, go one step down.

## Theme, density and language

The theme (system, dark, light, Blueprint) and the language change on the fly and are saved to your account. Density, "Comfortable" or "Compact", is kept in the browser. Theme and language can also be switched quickly from the account menu.
