## What it does

- **The basics** — table of contents and thumbnails, search ++ctrl+f++, zoom, print, “Download”; your reading position is remembered.
- **“GOST margins”** — guides over the page: you see what spilled past the margins; the **ruler** measures in millimetres.
- **Counters** — pages, prose words and characters; **night mode** — inversion for reading in the dark.
- **“Compare”** — against any past build: change highlighting, overlay, a slider curtain. You see what changed since the version your supervisor already read.
- **SyncTeX** — ++ctrl++ + click on a paragraph in the PDF opens the source at the right line, and back; select text to get “Ask AI”.
- **Signed downloads** — a PDF with stamped signatures and a cryptographic signature, if configured.
- **Non-PDF documents** — a live preview of reveal slides; for pptx — a converted PDF preview and “Download .pptx”.
