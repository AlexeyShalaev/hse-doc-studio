HSE Studio helps you format the documents of a thesis, coursework or Project Proposal to your
programme’s requirements: the template already has title pages, required sections and GOST checks,
and Studio builds a finished PDF from the sources. Start by signing in and the **Projects** screen;
everything else opens from a project.

<Steps />

## If something goes wrong

- **“Wrong email or password.”**: check the keyboard layout and case. If you forgot the password,
  click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen.
- **“Your account is awaiting administrator approval.”**: sign-in works once it is approved; you
  will get an email.
- **“Could not load projects”**: the server did not answer. Check your connection and click
  **Try again**; your projects are safe.
- **“Docker unavailable”** in the local Studio: start Docker Desktop, documents do not build
  without it. **Open settings** leads to the compiler settings.
