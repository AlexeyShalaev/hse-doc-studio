When a student sends documents for signing, the request goes to whoever’s turn it is. Until your
turn comes the tab is empty: the task appears together with a notification.

<Steps />

## If something goes wrong

- **A project invitation came, but no task**: accept the invitation with **Accept** first. If it is
  already your turn, the Studio opens the signing page right away; otherwise the task appears on the
  tab when your turn comes.
- **The task disappeared**: the route was cancelled or stopped, or you have already signed. You get
  a notification when the author sends again.
- **“A new version is out for signing” came, but no task**: the author changed a document you
  already approved or signed. The request comes when your turn does.
- **The author edits the document, but no notifications come**: this is expected. You sign the
  version sent for signing, and working edits send no notifications.
- **No “To sign” tab**: signing by route works only on a Studio server.
