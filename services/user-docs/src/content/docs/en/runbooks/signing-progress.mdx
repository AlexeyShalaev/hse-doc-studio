The route moves the documents from one signer to the next on its own. All you do is keep an eye on
the **Signing** card: hurry someone up if they are holding things up, and send a new version if you
fixed a document or it was returned for revision.

<Steps />

## If something goes wrong

- **“The route has stopped”** with a reason: a signer left the project, or the version for
  signing or the signatures on the pages changed outside the route (for example through git).
  Edits and rebuilds do not stop the route. Fix the reason on the route screen (**Open route**)
  and click **Send again** or **Send new version**.
- **“The version sent for signing cannot be restored: send a new version.”**: the route was sent
  on an earlier Studio version, and the sent PDF was not kept. Click **Send new version**: the
  current build becomes the version for signing.
- **Changes after a rebuild without edits**: this happens with documents from Word or PowerPoint,
  their PDF differs on every build. If the text did not change, you do not need to send a new
  version.
- **“0 of 3 signed” although you have approved**: this is expected. Sending the route counts as
  your approval, and the Studio asks for your e-signature after the PDF is frozen, once everyone
  has approved. The card shows both counts, “1 of 3 approved · 0 of 3 signed”, and your slot in the
  document list says “approved, signature after freeze”.
- **The order is wrong** after sending: click **Cancel route**, fix the steps and click **Send
  again**.
- **No “Remind” button**: you can remind only the person whose turn it is, or someone who has not
  accepted the invitation yet, and at most once every 12 hours. The button tooltip says when you
  can remind again.
- **The server does not send email**: requests still arrive in the Inbox. Send the link yourself
  with **Copy link**.
