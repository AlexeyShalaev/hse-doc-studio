Sometimes a signature is not needed at a checkpoint: for example, the supervisor approved the
application, and the academic supervisor's signature was added by yourself or turned out to be
redundant. To avoid cancelling the route, mark it optional and skip the person when their turn
comes.

<Steps />

## If something goes wrong

- **No “Skip” button**: only the person who manages the route can skip, and only when the
  person's turn has come and all their remaining signatures at this checkpoint are optional.
- **“There are required signatures: cannot skip”**: the person still has a required signature. If
  it is not needed, remove it from the route or mark it optional.
- **You cannot tick “Signature optional”**: the template requires this signature, or it is
  already in place.
- **Skipped by mistake**: clear **Signature optional** on the signature (the skipped person shows
  “Returns to the route on the next send”) and press **Send again**.
- **Skip automatically or over MCP**: not possible, this is the route author's decision in the
  interface.
- **The package has the line “not required (skipped by the author)”**: that is expected, it is a
  line of the `КАК_ПРОВЕРИТЬ.txt` guide. A skipped signature does not block the package.
