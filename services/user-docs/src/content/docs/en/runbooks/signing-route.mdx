A signing route is for when other people have to sign the checkpoint’s documents: the
supervisor, a co-supervisor, the academic supervisor. The Studio keeps the order itself: everyone
gets the request in their turn and signs on their own page.

Signers sign the version taken at sending. After sending you can still edit and build the
documents: the route goes on, and the corrected text goes to the signers with **Send new version**
on the **Signing** card.

<Steps />

## If something goes wrong

- **“document not built”** in **What blocks sending**: click **Build** in **Checkpoint documents**.
- **“signature not placed on the page”**: click **Go to placement** and place the frame with
  **To page N**.
- **“person is not in the project, invite them”**: click **Invite** next to the person. You can
  send before they accept: their step will wait.
- **“document is on the signing route of checkpoint “…””**: one document is signed by one route.
  Click **Open that checkpoint** and cancel the route there.
- **“your signature is not ready”**: upload an image or set a key in **Your signature**.
- There is no **Send for signing** button: the route works only on a Studio server, and only the
  project owner or an editor can send.
- The route is already sent and a document is fixed: nothing to change here. Click **Send new
  version** on the **Signing** card. To change the people or the order, cancel the route first.
