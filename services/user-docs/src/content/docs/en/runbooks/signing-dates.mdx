Templates often have a date line next to a signature, for example “« ___ » ________ 20__”. Instead
of writing the date by hand or printing the document, put date marks on the signature: the
Studio fills in the signing day.

<Steps />

## If something goes wrong

- **The marks are empty**: the signature in the route is not approved yet, or the signature
  outside a route has no **Signing date**.
- **No “Signing date” field**: the signature is in the route, its date comes from the approval
  (“The date comes from the approval in the signing route”). You cannot set it by hand.
- **The date differs from the time of the electronic signature**: that is expected. The page shows
  the approval day, the time of the signature itself is in the package guide (`HOW_TO_VERIFY.txt`, `КАК_ПРОВЕРИТЬ.txt` in a Russian project).
- **“The document has no page N: the date will not reach the PDF”**: the document got shorter,
  for example after a template update. Move the mark to the open page with **Here**.
- **An e-signature signer now has 2 actions**: the date mark needs their approval before the
  final PDF is frozen. Put them last among the people who add visible signatures, and they
  approve and sign with one click again.
- **Adding marks over MCP**: not possible, the agent only reads them.
