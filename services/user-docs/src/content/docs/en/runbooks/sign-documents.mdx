A link to this page comes by email and in the Inbox on the **To sign** tab. The page opens only
after you sign in to your account, so a forwarded email does not let anyone sign for you. It also
works in a phone browser: the buttons stick to the bottom of the screen.

<Steps />

## If something goes wrong

- **“The document changed while the page was open”**: the author rebuilt the document. Look at the
  new version and click the button again.
- **“The document changed after your approval: approve again”**: the author fixed the document and
  sent it again. Your image and key are in place, so it is one click.
- **“The route has stopped”**: the author is editing the document or changing the signers. You get
  a notification when they send again.
- **“Signing page not found”**: the route is closed, the link is outdated or you signed in to
  another account. Open the Inbox, **To sign** tab.
- **No “Image” method**: the author did not place your signature on the document page, so only the
  checkpoint’s method is left.
- **CryptoPro on a phone** does not work: the browser plug-in exists only on a computer. On a phone,
  sign with an image or a Studio key.
